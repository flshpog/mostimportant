Tom Nook's Tax Returns
Thank you for your purchase! This item operates on a "one-time use" kind of ruling. At any point within the next 24 hours, ping Production and let them know you are ready to activate it. It will expire after 24 hours, so keep that in mind! You'll be given a visual consisting of the inventories of all players in the game, except you won't know whose inventory is whose! Note that the Tax Returns cannot detect an in-server Hidden Immunity Idol.			
			
# **Resetti's Do-Over**				
Thank you for your purchase! This item can be used for any individual challenge up until the Final 6. You may play it after your first run of a challenge to essentially get a second chance. You'll ping Production and let them know you're playing your advantage. From there, you will be given further instructions by a Producer and be granted one more shot at the challenge. You'll keep the better score. For challenges with set answers, your second run will be an alternate set meaning you will not get to play a challenge on "god mode". Note that it will be public that you used this, whether you won the challenge or not.			

# **Redd's Counterfeit**				
Thank you for your purchase! You may hang onto this item for as long as you have it, it will not expire. When you are ready to use it, you'll ping Production and select a target as well as the right to transform the Counterfeit into any advantage in the game! Could be something from Nook's Cranny or you can even attempt to fake a Hidden Immunity Idol. The target will not know their advantage is fake until they stand up at Tribal Council to play it. Note when that happens, it will be formally revealed that YOU played this advantage on THEM.

# **Isabelle's Briefing**				
Thank you for your purchase! This item will immediately earn you a trip to Isabelle at the front desk. She will brief you on any previous Tribal Council of the season. You'll receive a visual graphic depicting who everyone has voted for along with what advantages were played and by whom. Isabelle will also catch you up to speed on what (if any) alliance chats were created during the Tribal Council, as well as the AVERAGES on Trust Rankings from each player who attended the Tribal Council. Note that you cannot hold this for any eliminations that occured after this purchase.			
			
# **Kappn's Island Tour**				
Thank you for your purchase! This item will immediately grant you temporary access to another tribe's zone for the rest of the current episode (or next, depending on when this bought). You'll be able to view and speak in camp chat as well as Tribal if they attend. You will be able to form alliances with the players on that tribe, but you can't use 1-1s. Once the cycle is up, if you would like to mutiny to the tribe you toured, you may do so, for the cost of an additional 2,000 bells. You'll be granted Individual Immunity for that episode. Note that you cannot cast votes for another tribe or impact another tribe's challenge score while touring.

# **Golden Axe**				
Thank you for your purchase! This item acts as a buffed Steal-A-Vote that is to be played during the advantage call (not pre-played). On top of being able to cast an extra vote, you will select one player and you will get to steal their vote- in this instance, their vote will not be read and there will be two additional parchments submitted by you. One parchment is the extra vote that comes with this advantage and the other is the "stolen vote" of the player who you have played this advantage on. Additional note, if the player targetted by the Steal-A-Vote did not vote at Tribal, than this advantage does nothing and is returned to its holder.
